*Last updated 24 November 2026.*

Straatwijsheid is a hobby project and an open dataset. It collects as little as possible.

## Who is responsible

Marius Strom is responsible for the data described here (the "controller" under the GDPR/AVG). To reach me, [open an issue](https://github.com/straatwijsheid/website/issues/new) or use the contribution form once it is live. I do not publish a postal address.

## Visiting the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives your IP address when you load a page, as any host does, and handles it under [its own privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

I measure visits with [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/). It sets no cookies and does not track you across sites or build a profile of you. I see page views, referring sites, countries, browsers and page-speed figures in aggregate. Your IP address is not stored in the statistics I see.

The map is loaded in a frame from `maps.straatwijsheid.nl`. It fetches map tiles from OpenStreetMap-based servers, which receive your IP address and the area you are viewing.

The site uses no advertising and no social-media trackers. Your light or dark preference is kept in your browser only.

## Proposing a change or reporting a problem

On GitHub, what you write is public and governed by [GitHub's terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy). A contribution form is planned for people without a GitHub account. When it is live this page will be updated, and it will work like this:

- Your proposal or report becomes a **public** issue in the [knowledge repository](https://github.com/straatwijsheid/knowledge). Do not put anything private in it.
- An email address is optional. If you give one, it is **not** put in the public issue. It is stored privately for up to 90 days so I can reply, then deleted automatically.
- The form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to keep out spam. Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser signals for that purpose.
- By contributing you agree your answer may be published under CC-BY 4.0, credited as you wish.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and to object to its use. Contact me as above and mention the issue number if there is one. I will delete a stored email address on request. A public GitHub issue can only be removed by a repository administrator, so ask and I will delete it. Copies others made or cached before then are outside my control.

If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the [Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl).

## The data itself

The street-name dataset is built from public sources (OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, BAG and others) and describes streets, not people living on them. See [sources and licences](/docs/sources-and-licences/).
