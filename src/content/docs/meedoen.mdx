You can help name the streets the map could not identify, or correct one it got wrong.

**With GitHub:** read [how to fill in a review sheet](/docs/reviewing/) and [how to contribute](/docs/contributing/), then open a pull request on a gemeente's review sheet.

**Without GitHub:** use the form below to propose a change or report a problem. A volunteer will check it before anything changes on the map.

<ContributeForm />

By contributing you agree your answer may be published under CC-BY 4.0, credited as you wish.

See the [privacy page](/privacy/) for what is stored about you.
