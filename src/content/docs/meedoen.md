---
title: Take part
description: Propose a correction or report a problem, with or without a GitHub account.
---

You can help name the streets the map could not identify, or correct one it got wrong.

**With GitHub:** see the [reviewing guide](https://github.com/straatwijsheid/knowledge/blob/main/REVIEWING.md) and open a pull request on a gemeente's review sheet.

**Without GitHub:** a form for proposing a change or reporting a problem is coming. Until then, open the map, click a street and follow its *Help name it* link.

By contributing you agree your answer may be published under CC-BY 4.0, credited as you wish.
